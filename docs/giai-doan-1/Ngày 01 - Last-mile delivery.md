# Ngày 01 — Last-mile delivery & order batching

- Chủ đề: Logistics
- Thời lượng: 30 phút
- Mục tiêu: (1) nhận diện signal words để tìm "khung xương" của đoạn văn; (2) tìm được trade-off mà tác giả nêu ra.
- Quy tắc: KHÔNG dùng công cụ dịch. Đoán nghĩa từ ngữ cảnh trước.

## Bài đọc (~190 từ)

Last-mile delivery is the final step of the delivery process, in which a package is moved from a local distribution center to the customer's door. Although it covers the shortest distance, it is often the most expensive part of the entire journey. Drivers must stop at many different addresses, and each stop usually involves only one or two packages.

One common strategy for reducing this cost is order batching. Instead of sending a vehicle for each order, the system groups several orders that are going to nearby addresses and assigns them to a single route. As a result, each driver can deliver more packages per trip, and fuel consumption per package decreases.

However, batching comes at the cost of delivery speed. To build an efficient batch, the system must wait until enough orders have arrived, which means that some customers receive their packages later. Therefore, logistics companies need to decide how long the system should wait before dispatching a vehicle. A longer waiting window usually leads to lower cost, while a shorter one improves customer satisfaction.
